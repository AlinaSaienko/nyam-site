nyam. Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data nyam. collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It applies to the nyam. iPhone app and its related emails and website.

In short

  • We collect what you give us (profile, body details, meals, photos) and what you allow (Apple Health, camera, reminders).
  • Some of it is health data. We use it only to run nyam., and only with your consent.
  • You choose who sees your plates. Alcohol, allergies, body details, weight and Apple Health data are always private to you.
  • We do not sell your personal data, show ads, or use tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time. Everything is erased straight away.

Who we are

nyam. is made and run by Alina Saienko, an independent developer acting as an individual, not a company ("nyam.", "we", "us"). I decide how your data is used and I am responsible for it.

Photos, AI and safety checks

  • Food recognition. When you snap a photo or describe a meal, the photo (cropped to the plate), the plate or bowl size and your text are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic (its Claude models), to find foods and estimate grams and calories. They go through our own server, so Anthropic does not receive your name, email or username.
  • Activity check. If you ask nyam. to check whether your activity level fits, we send an average of your recent Apple Health activity (steps, active energy, exercise minutes, workouts per week and their types) together with your sex, age, weight, goal and chosen level. Nothing else from Apple Health is sent.
  • No training. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, content sent through its API is not used to train its models. Anthropic may keep it for a limited time under its own policies.
  • On your device. Finding the plate rim and scanning barcodes happen on your phone.
  • Photo check. Every photo is checked before it is saved or shared for nudity, sexual content, violence, gore and other graphic content. This check is part of the same AI request. If a photo fails, it is not saved or shown to anyone. Only if you ask for a review is it kept, privately, so a person can look at it.
  • Text check. Captions and comments are checked for slurs and spam links.
  • Reports. If someone reports your post, I may look at the post and your account activity to decide what to do.
  • Voice. "Describe it" uses your keyboard's own dictation. nyam. does not ask for microphone access.

AI estimates can be wrong. See the Terms of Use.

What other people can see

  • Always visible to anyone who finds you: your username, display name, avatar, bio and counts of plates, followers and friends.
  • Plates: only the people you choose in Settings → Privacy & safety (No one, Friends only, Everyone, or Selected people). The default is Friends only. You can show or hide kcal and macros on posts and decide who may comment.
  • Awards: shown to the people you choose, Friends only by default. Awards you are still working on are only ever visible to you.
  • Recipes: shown to the people you choose when you publish.
  • Never shown to others: body details and measurements, weight, BMI, goals, allergies, alcohol, Apple Health data, the shopping list, and your reports.
  • Blocked people cannot find you, see your plates or comment, and are not told that you blocked them.

What you share with others may be seen, screenshotted or saved by them. We cannot control copies they make.

How long we keep data, and deleting your account

  • While you have an account we keep your data so the app works.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete my account. nyam. immediately and permanently deletes your profile, meals, photos, posts, likes, follows, favourites and the rest of your data. This cannot be undone and there is no waiting period.
  • Delete a meal, post or comment and it leaves the app straight away. A deleted meal is kept hidden for up to 30 days so that Undo works, then erased.
  • Photos that fail the safety check are not stored. A photo you ask us to review is kept privately only until the review is done.
  • The AI request log and safety records are kept only while your account exists and go with it when you delete it.
  • Backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten on its normal schedule, so a copy can remain in them for a short time after deletion.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access your data and get a copy.
  • Correct data that is wrong. Most of it you can edit yourself in the app.
  • Delete your data. The fastest way is to delete your account in the app.
  • Restrict or object to some uses of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example by turning off Apple Health or reminders, or by removing body details. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), the law may give you these rights in a specific form, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Email [contact email]. We reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm it is you. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Children, transfers abroad, security and changes

Children. nyam. is for people aged 13 and over, or older where your country's law sets a higher age for using an app like this. Calorie and weight tracking is not suitable for young people, so if you are under 18, use nyam. only with a parent or guardian's agreement. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we will delete it. If you think a child is using nyam., write to [contact email].

Transfers. nyam. is available worldwide. Our providers, including Supabase and Anthropic, may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where the law requires it, they rely on safeguards such as the Standard Contractual Clauses.

Security. Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access rules, so one person cannot read another's private data. Photos are kept in private storage. Sign-in uses one-time codes or Apple, not passwords. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Changes. If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will tell you in the app before it applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.
