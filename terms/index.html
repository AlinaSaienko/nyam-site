  • Not medical advice. nyam. is a tracking and information tool. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before you change your diet or exercise, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, take medication, or have or have had an eating disorder.
  • Estimates, not measurements. Calories, macros, portions, burned calories, BMI, forecasts and goals are estimates based on what you enter, on food databases and on AI. They can be wrong. Check them, and edit them when they look off.

AI features

nyam. uses AI (Claude, from Anthropic) to recognise food in photos, to turn a description into items, to suggest recipe titles, amounts and steps, to check photos against the community rules, and, if you ask, to check whether your activity level fits your Apple Health data. AI can make mistakes: it can miss or misname foods, misjudge portions, and miss hidden ingredients such as nuts. Always review the result before you save or publish it, and edit anything that looks wrong. Suggested recipes are drafts that you are responsible for before you publish them.

To keep the app free and costs fair, AI features have daily limits per person (for example about 30 photo recognitions a day). If you reach a limit you can still log by search, barcode, description or quick add, and it resets the next day. We may change the limits.

What the AI receives and how it is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.
