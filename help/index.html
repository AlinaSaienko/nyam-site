nyam. Help

Last updated 3 November 2026

nyam. helps you log what you eat in seconds, see your day at a glance and share your plates with friends. This guide explains every part of the app.

Getting started

You can sign in with Apple or with your email, and the first sign-in takes you through a short setup.

  1. Sign in. Tap Sign in with Apple, or enter your email and then the 6-digit code we send you. There are no passwords. The code works for 10 minutes. If it is wrong or expired, tap Resend or Send a new code.
  2. Profile. Pick a username (we check it is free), add an avatar and a short bio if you like. Both are optional.
  3. Your goal. Choose Lose, Maintain or Gain weight, or Build muscle. For losing or gaining, pick a pace: Gentle (0.25 kg a week), Steady (0.5) or Faster (0.75). Then pick your activity level.
  4. About you. Sex, age, height, weight and, optionally, goal weight. Only you see these. A BMI card updates as you type.
  5. Sizes and measurements (optional). Clothes sizes and body measurements, so you can see change over time. Tap Skip if you prefer.
  6. Daily goal. nyam. suggests a calorie goal and explains it in one line. You can adjust it in steps of 50. It also suggests a water goal and a macro split (Balanced by default).
  7. Meals. Choose how many meals you eat each day (4 by default) and name them. Limits per meal are Auto by default, or you can set them by hand.

The suggested goal never goes below 1,200 kcal (female), 1,500 kcal (male) or 1,350 kcal (other). You can set a lower number yourself, but nyam. will warn you, and the lowest it allows is 1,000 kcal. Below those levels it is hard to get enough nutrients, so only go there with a doctor's advice.

Logging a meal

Every + or log button opens Log a meal. Pick the meal at the top (it is chosen by time of day), then choose how to log it.

Way What it does
Snap a photo The AI finds the foods and estimates grams and kcal.
Search food Search by name; results show kcal per 100 g. Set the amount and add.
Scan barcode Reads a packaged product and its label data. Pick a serving or grams.
Describe it Type or dictate, for example "2 eggs and toast". The AI turns it into items for you to check.
Quick add Calories only, with an optional name and macros.

Below the buttons, the Recent, Frequent and Favourites tabs let you log a past meal with one tap on +.

Photos and plate size

The AI needs your plate's real size to judge portions.

  • Pick Small (about 20 cm), Medium (about 25 cm) or Large (about 28 cm). Medium is the default and nyam. remembers your choice.
  • Shoot from straight above, at any distance. nyam. snaps a purple circle to the rim. If it cannot find the rim, the shutter waits.
  • Zoom with a pinch or the .5 / 1× / 2× buttons. Only a square around the plate is kept and shared.
  • After the shutter, check the circle on the rim (drag or pinch if it is off), confirm the size and tap Looks right.
  • Photos from your library work the same way: place the circle on the rim, pick a size, tap Use this photo.
  • Switch to Bowl for soups and stews. Pick the width (Small about 12 cm, Medium 15, Large 18), the depth (Shallow, Medium, Deep) and how full it is, and add an optional side photo.

Tap ? on the camera at any time for a short explanation.

Checking and editing the result

Every item can be edited, including those the AI found. Tap the pencil to change the name, the grams (10 g steps, kcal and macros follow), or the kcal by hand, or to remove it. An item the AI is unsure about shows Check this. Then tap Log lunch (or whichever meal) to save. Toggle Share to feed to post it to friends.

No photo? You can log a meal without one, but it cannot be shared, because feed posts always have a photo. Tap the star to save a meal to Favourites.

Your day on Home

  • Plates row. Round photos of new plates from friends and people you follow. A purple ring means new from a friend, a light ring means new from someone you follow, a faint ring means seen. Tap one to open it full screen and swipe through.
  • Today card. The calorie ring and your macros (Protein, Carbs, Fat). Tap the card to open Analytics.
  • Over your goal. The ring and bars shade towards red and the centre shows how many kcal over you are. Red only ever means you are over a limit or something is wrong.
  • Meal limits. Each meal has a kcal limit. A meal turns red only when it passes its limit by more than 10%, or by any amount once the rest of the day's budget is used up.
  • My meals. Your meals today, with likes and comments. Tap a meal to open it.
  • Change day. Swipe left or right, or use the arrows. Past days show On target or Over by N%. Tomorrow lets you plan meals. Back to today returns you.
  • Calendar. Tap the date. Days are coloured: on target (up to 10% over goal), over (10–25%) and well over (more than 25%), always with a label. The Streak view shows your logging streak.
  • Streak. A day counts when you log any meal or drink. One empty day resets it.
  • Edit or delete a meal. Swipe a meal left for Edit or Delete, or open it and use the buttons. A private meal is deleted at once with an Undo for 5 seconds. A shared meal asks first, because its post, likes and comments go too. To keep the meal but remove the post, edit it and turn off Share to feed.

Water, drinks and alcohol

  • Water. The water card on Home shows litres against your goal (suggested at about 30 ml per kg of body weight). Tap + 250 ml to add a cup, or Other drink to log something else. You can change the cup size (200, 250, 330 or 500 ml) and the goal in Settings → Meals, drinks & limits.
  • Other drinks. Choose a drink and an amount in Log a drink. Drinks sold by volume are saved as drinks: their calories count in your daily total as liquid calories and their volume counts towards water. Tea and coffee count towards water by default; you can turn that off.
  • Alcohol. Choose Log alcohol, then the type, size and strength. nyam. shows standard drinks, grams of alcohol, calories and your week against your weekly limit (default 7 standard drinks). Alcohol never counts as water.
  • Alcohol is always private. It is never shown on posts, profiles or to friends, and a meal with alcohol is posted without it.

If you are worried about your drinking, talk to a doctor.

Allergies and diet

In Settings → Goals & body, add your allergies and diet. Only you see this list.

Level What you see
Allergy A red alert with an icon and words
Intolerance An orange warning
Avoid A grey note

You can pick from 14 common allergens, add your own ingredients, and choose a diet (vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, no pork, no alcohol).

When a logged meal contains an allergen, a banner such as "Contains peanuts: you're allergic" appears and sharing is hidden. You can remove the item in one tap or choose Log anyway. Scanned products use the label's "contains" and "may contain" data. Recipes show an info chip.

Please check labels yourself. Photos can miss hidden ingredients, and nyam. cannot guarantee that a food is safe for you.

Activity and Apple Health

nyam. can read your workouts, steps, active energy, walking, running, cycling and swimming distance, exercise minutes and body weight from Apple Health. You choose what to share in the iOS permission prompt, and you can change it at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

  • Activity stack. Today's activities appear under the Today card, with the latest on top. Each shows its name, the best metric (steps, distance and time, or time) and calories burned.
  • Add an activity by hand. Enter the type, duration, distance and calories. If you allow it, nyam. saves it to Apple Health too, and the same goes for weight entries.
  • Burned calories. nyam. estimates your resting energy from your age, height, weight and sex, and adds active energy from Apple Health. You can choose whether burned calories are added to your daily budget in Settings → App.
  • Activity check. After at least 5 days of data, you can ask nyam. whether your activity level fits what you really do. For this, an average of your recent activity, with your sex, age, weight and goal, is sent to our AI provider. Nothing else from Apple Health is sent. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Smart scale. Weigh-ins from a connected scale arrive through Apple Health.

If you denied access, nyam. shows a link to the iOS settings so you can turn it on.

Friends, plates and privacy

  • Follow people. Use Find people to search by username or share your invite link. If you follow each other, you are friends.
  • Who sees your plates. Settings → Privacy & safety. Choose No one, Friends only (the default), Everyone or Selected people. Your profile, bio and counts stay visible to anyone who finds you; this setting controls plates only. People who cannot see your plates see a profile without them.
  • On your posts. Turn kcal and macros on or off, and choose who can comment.
  • Comment and like. Open a plate in the Plates viewer or the Feed to like, comment or star it. Starring someone's meal saves a copy to your Favourites for one-tap logging.
  • Hide, report or block. Tap … on someone else's post. Hiding removes it from your view. To report, pick a reason and add details if you want; the person is not told it was you, and we aim to review reports within 24 hours. Blocking hides you from each other and removes follows both ways without notifying them. Manage blocked accounts and see your reports in Settings → Privacy & safety.
  • If your post is removed. You see which rule it broke. The meal still counts in your day, and you can ask for a review or delete the photo.

If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services.

Recipes, Saved and the shopping list

  • Browse recipes. In Feed → Recipes, filter by Under 20 min, High protein, Under 500 kcal or Saved. Change servings and every amount scales.
  • Log a serving. Tap Log 1 serving to open the review with the recipe's items.
  • Make a recipe. Tap + Recipe. Snap your ingredients, start from one of your plates, or write it yourself. The AI suggests a title, amounts and steps, all marked as suggestions. Nothing is public until you tap Edit & publish. Choose who sees it and whether to also post it to the feed.
  • Saved. The star in the header opens everything you starred, in two tabs: Recipes and Meals. Tap a filled star to unsave, and use Undo if it was a mistake.
  • Shopping list. The basket icon shows how many items are left to buy. Add a recipe from the … menu or after saving it: choose servings and untick what you already have. Items already on the list are added together. Tick items as you buy them, then tap Done shopping to keep what is left or clear the list. The list is private and syncs across your devices.

Analytics and awards

Analytics opens from the tab bar or by tapping the Today card. Switch between Week, Month and Year, and tap any card for detail.

Screen What it shows
Calories Days against your goal, by meal, by hour, weekdays against weekends, top foods
Macros Daily grams, average against target, protein per kg
Energy balance Eaten minus burned, deficit in kcal and kg
Activity Steps, active time, active kcal, workouts
Weight & BMI Weigh-ins, 7-day trend, forecast to your goal
Body Measurements and clothes sizes over time
Water & drinks Litres against goal, liquid calories
Alcohol Week against your limit (only if you track it)

BMI is weight in kg divided by height in metres squared. It is shown on a neutral scale with no colour judgement, and it is only a rough guide.

Awards are on your profile. They are tied to things you do in the app: streaks, goals, activity, water, body, social and exploring. Calorie awards only count when you eat enough, because eating less is never rewarded, and there are no awards for how fast you lose weight. Earned awards are never taken away. Choose who sees them in Settings → Privacy & safety → Awards.

Account and settings

Open your profile and tap the cog.

Group What you can change
Goals & body Daily goal (with Reset to suggested), goal and activity, body and measurements, meals, drinks and limits, allergies and diet
Social Privacy & safety, Notifications
App Apple Health, Units (kg · cm or lb · ft), add burned calories to my budget
Account Sign-in, Sign out, Delete account
  • Edit profile. Change your avatar, name, username and bio (150 characters, links allowed).
  • Notifications. Choose each type, set water and meal reminders, the Monday weekly summary and quiet hours. Safety messages always appear in the list.
  • Delete account. Settings → Account → Delete account. Your profile, meals, photos, posts, follows and favourites are deleted straight away and for good. This cannot be undone.

Troubleshooting and contact

Problem What to try
My code does not work Codes last 10 minutes and work once. Tap Resend, check spam, and use the newest email.
"No food found" Retake the photo from straight above in good light, search for the food, or add it manually.
"You're offline" Your photo and plate size are kept. Tap Try again when you are back online.
"We can't use this photo" Photos are checked before saving. Take another photo, log without a photo, or ask for a review.
Portions look wrong Check the plate or bowl size, and edit the grams on the review screen.
A product is not found Search by name or add it from the label.
No activity from Apple Health Check iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
I logged something by mistake Swipe the meal left and tap Delete, then Undo within 5 seconds if needed.
"Daily limit reached" AI features have daily limits (about 30 photo recognitions a day). Log by search, barcode, description or quick add, and try again tomorrow.
